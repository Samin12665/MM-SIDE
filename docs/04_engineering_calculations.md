| Traction-limited torque/rear wheel (μ 0.9) | 0.400 N·m |
| Encoder resolution at wheel | 64 × 19 = 1216 counts/rev → 0.220 mm/count |

Assessment: a 2 m/s top speed is deliberate — it keeps camera motion blur, LiDAR scan skew and stopping distance compatible with indoor SLAM and path-planning work. Hill climbing at 15° uses 33 % of stall torque, which is inside the gearmotor's intermittent envelope; continuous operation should stay below ~25 % stall. Traction limit (0.40 N·m) is below stall (0.77 N·m), so an uncontrolled full-throttle launch can spin the rear tyres and corrupt wheel odometry. Firmware must ramp-limit acceleration to ≤ 2 m/s² (0.20 N·m, 51 % of traction limit); the front AS5600 wheel encoders (non-driven wheels) provide an independent slip check.

Stopping distance from top speed at 3 m/s² (active braking by the H-bridge): **573 mm**.

Gearmotor output shaft (Ø6 mm D-shaft) under a 3 g bump: F = 31.4 N at 18 mm overhang → M = 565 N·mm, σ = 27 MPa (steel yield ≥ 350 MPa, SF > 9). Wheel hub is placed hard against the gearbox face to keep the overhang short.

## 4.3 Steering

* Layout: front-steer dual bellcranks on vertical M3 shoulder bolts, drag link, M3 turnbuckle tie rods (74.3 mm).
* Inner / outer lock: **26.0° / 19.5°** — Ackermann 102 % at lock, 91 % at mid-travel (100 % = ideal).
* Turning radius (rear-axle centre): **0.72 m**; kerb-to-kerb (outer front tyre) ≈ 1.02 m.
* Bump steer over -12…+18 mm: **0.02°** (tie-rod inner ball placed on the instant-centre line; hold crank height ±0.2 mm with shims).
* Static (parked) steering torque per kingpin, μ=1.0, r_eff = scrub 12 + patch 10 mm: 0.219 N·m.
* Required servo torque ≈ **0.44 N·m (4.5 kg·cm)**. Selected 20 kg·cm servo → safety factor **4.4**.
* Crank rotation to lock: 31.0° → servo throw ≈ ±28° (within standard ±45°).

* Front lower arm, 5 g, load at ball 33 mm outboard of shock mount: M = 1639 N·mm, section 18×8 mm → σ = 8.5 MPa. PA-CF (Z-direction-free layup, printed flat) allowable ≈ 45 MPa → SF 5.3.
* Rear trailing arm, 5 g: M = 1883 N·mm at shock mount → σ = 1.3 MPa (beam 18×22 mm) → SF 34.7. Motor torque reaction (0.8 N·m stall) is carried by 6× M3 face bolts: shear per bolt 9 N.
* Chassis spine: 2 × 10×10×1 6063 tubes (I = 492 mm⁴ each) along the full tub (238 mm, rear-arm pivots → front bulkhead joint). Conservative case — whole 3 g vehicle load at mid-span on the rails alone, simply supported: deflection 0.51 mm, stress 37.0 MPa vs 6063-T5 yield 145 MPa. The 55 mm deep printed tub walls and the bolted deck act in parallel and carry most of this in practice.
* Front bulkhead is a cantilever off the tub front wall (6× M3 into inserts + flange bearing). A 5 g front-corner load on both sides (99 N) at ≈ 35 mm from the flange gives ≈ 3.5 N·m → ≈ 70 N tension in the upper bolt pair, far below the ≈ 800 N pull-out of an M3 insert in PETG-CF.
* Torsion is carried by the closed box formed by skid + tub walls + rails + upper deck (deck screwed at 8 points).
